# Operating rhythm

A business runs on a predictable cadence of short meetings, each with a fixed input and a fixed output. Departments plug their SOPs into this rhythm, and agents prepare the inputs (A0/A1) so the meetings spend their time on decisions.

| Cadence | Meeting | Length | Input (agent can prepare) | Output | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily | Team stand-up (optional) | 10–15 min | Each person's top priorities, blockers | Unblocked work | Department heads |
| Weekly | Department review, e.g. Sales pipeline review ([SAL-SOP-005](../sales/sops/SAL-SOP-005-pipeline-review-and-forecast.md)) | 30–60 min | KPI snapshot, exceptions, at-risk items | Decisions and owners for each exception | Department heads |
| Weekly | Leadership sync | 30–60 min | One-page update per department: KPIs vs target, top 3 risks, asks | Cross-department decisions | owner-ceo |
| Monthly | Business review | 60–90 min | Monthly KPI scorecard, financials, forecast, win/loss themes ([SAL-SOP-008](../sales/sops/SAL-SOP-008-win-loss-review.md)) | Course corrections, budget changes | owner-ceo |
| Quarterly | Planning | half day | Last quarter's results, customer and market input, capacity | Goals and KPI targets for next quarter, SOP changes | owner-ceo |
| Quarterly | SOP review | 30 min per department | SOPs due for review, incidents, agent error log | Updated SOP versions, autonomy changes | SOP owners |

## Rules that keep meetings useful

1. **No meeting without its input.** If the scorecard isn't ready, the meeting is moved, not improvised.
2. **Exceptions, not recitals.** Review what's off target or at risk. Skip what's on track.
3. **Every decision gets an owner and a date,** recorded where the team works (task tool, CRM, shared doc).
4. **One number per goal.** Each goal for the quarter has a single KPI that says whether it was met.
