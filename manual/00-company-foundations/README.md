## How to adopt this manual

1. **Fill in your business context.** It's the single biggest factor in output quality.
2. **Map roles to people.** In a small business one person holds many roles. That's expected. The RACI charts still tell you which hat they're wearing at each step.
3. **Pick one department and one or two SOPs** that hurt most today. Run them as written for two weeks, then adapt the wording to your business.
4. **Introduce agents at A1.** Let them draft while people act. Track where drafts needed heavy edits, and fix the SOP or business context so the next draft is better.
5. **Review quarterly.** Update SOPs, raise or lower autonomy, retire what you don't use.
