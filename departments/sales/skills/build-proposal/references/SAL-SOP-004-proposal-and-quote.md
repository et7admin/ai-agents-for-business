# SAL-SOP-004 Proposal and quote

## Purpose
Put a clear, accurate offer in front of a buyer who is ready for it, priced within policy, so it can be approved fast and delivered as sold.

## Scope
**In:** proposals, quotes and statements of work for new and expansion deals; pricing within policy; requesting approvals; negotiation.
**Out:** setting prices or the discount policy (owner-ceo), drafting or changing legal contract terms (legal-counsel), signing (a hard stop: the authorized person signs).

## Trigger
Stage 3 exit criteria are met: the solution fits the stated pain, feasibility is confirmed and success criteria are agreed with the buyer.

## Procedure

| # | Step | Role | Autonomy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm readiness: Stage 3 exit criteria met, and the decision-makers will attend a proposal review | account-executive | A0 | Readiness check |
| 2 | Confirm scope, deliverables, assumptions and timeline with the solutions-engineer or delivery lead | account-executive | A1 | Confirmed scope |
| 3 | Price it from the price list and discount policy, and flag anything outside policy | account-executive | A1 | Price build with flags |
| 4 | Request approval for each exception from the right approver (matrix below) | account-executive | A1 | Approval request |
| 5 | Draft the proposal using the structure below | account-executive | A1 | Draft proposal |
| 6 | Check the draft: prices match the approved build, scope matches delivery's confirmation, nothing is promised that isn't approved | sales-manager | A0 | Checked draft |
| 7 | Send the proposal and book a live review meeting | account-executive | A2 | Proposal sent; meeting booked |
| 8 | Walk the buyer through it live. Confirm fit, answer questions, agree next steps. | account-executive | A0 | Buyer feedback |
| 9 | Log every ask (price, terms, scope) and route it to the right approver. Trade, don't give. | account-executive | A1 | Negotiation log |
| 10 | When agreed, prepare the final quote or order form for signature by an authorized person | account-executive | A1 | Final paperwork |

## Proposal structure
1. **Executive summary:** their goal and problem, in their words, and what they'll get. One page at most.
2. **The problem and its impact:** from discovery, with their numbers.
3. **Proposed solution:** what we'll do and why it solves the problem.
4. **Scope and deliverables:** specific and countable, with what's out of scope.
5. **Timeline:** milestones, confirmed by delivery.
6. **Investment:** price, payment terms and options (if any). Show value, not just cost.
7. **Assumptions and dependencies:** what we need from them.
8. **Why us:** two or three proof points relevant to *their* problem.
9. **Next steps:** dates and who does what to start.

## Negotiation rules
- **Trade, don't give.** Every concession gets something back: longer term, upfront payment, a faster signature date, a case study, a larger scope.
- **Concede in decreasing steps** and never on the first ask.
- **Never negotiate against yourself.** Ask what they need before offering anything.
- **Keep it in writing.** Every agreed change goes into the negotiation log and the final paperwork.

## Decision rules
- No proposal goes out before Stage 3 exit criteria are met unless the sales-manager agrees.
- No price, discount or term goes to the buyer, even verbally, before its approval.
- Always present the proposal live. Emailing it without a review meeting is the exception, and needs a reason.
- A proposal is valid for the period stated in it (e.g. 30 days). After that it needs re-approval.

## Quality checks
- [ ] Every number matches the approved price build.
- [ ] The scope is confirmed by the people who'll deliver it.
- [ ] The executive summary uses the buyer's own goals and words.
- [ ] Every exception has a recorded approval with name and date.
- [ ] A review meeting is booked.

## Exceptions & escalation
| Situation | What to do | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer asks for terms nobody can approve | Say what we can do instead | head-of-sales |
| Buyer sends their own contract | Don't edit it. Send it for review. | legal-counsel |
| A competitor's price is quoted | Ask for specifics, re-establish value, then decide with an approver | sales-manager |
| Procurement asks for security or compliance questionnaires | Route them to the owners. Don't answer from memory. | head-of-it, legal-counsel |

## Records
Price build, approvals, proposal versions, negotiation log, final paperwork. Kept with the opportunity in the CRM or document store.
