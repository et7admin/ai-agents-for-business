# SAL-SOP-007 CRM data hygiene

## Purpose
Keep the CRM accurate enough to run the business on. Every forecast, KPI and agent in this department is only as good as the CRM data underneath it.

## Scope
**In:** sales records in the CRM: leads, contacts, companies, opportunities and activities.
**Out:** marketing automation data and customer support tickets (they have their own owners), CRM configuration changes beyond picklists and fields (an IT/CRM-admin change).

## Trigger
Logging happens after every interaction. The audit runs weekly, the working day before the pipeline review ([SAL-SOP-005](https://github.com/et7admin/ai-agents-for-business/blob/main/manual/sales/sops/SAL-SOP-005-pipeline-review-and-forecast.md)).

## Required data

**Every open opportunity**
- Owner, company, primary contact with role
- Stage matching the [exit criteria](https://github.com/et7admin/ai-agents-for-business/blob/main/manual/sales/README.md#standard-pipeline-stages)
- Close date in the future
- Next step (specific) with a date in the future
- Amount, from Stage 2 onward
- Lead source

**Additionally from Stage 3:** at least two engaged contacts, economic buyer identified, competitor or alternative recorded.

**Every closed-lost opportunity:** a loss reason from the standard list, and a one-line note.

**Standard loss reasons:** No decision / stayed with status quo · Lost to competitor · Price or budget · Timing / project delayed · Missing capability or poor fit · Champion left or reorganization · Disqualified by us.

## Procedure

| # | Step | Role | Autonomy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Log every call, meeting and meaningful email within 24 hours: what happened, what's next, and the next step date | account-executive | A3 | Activities |
| 2 | Run the weekly audit: required fields, past dates, missing next steps, stale deals, duplicates, placeholder values | sales-operations | A0 | Audit report |
| 3 | Send each record owner their list of fixes, worst first | sales-operations | A1 | Fix lists sent |
| 4 | Owners fix their records before the pipeline review | account-executive | A2 | Records fixed |
| 5 | Review suspected duplicates and merge the true ones | sales-operations | A1 | Duplicates resolved |
| 6 | For stale deals (no activity in 30 days or flagged three weeks running), the owner decides: re-engage with a dated plan, or close lost with a reason | account-executive | A1 | Decision recorded |
| 7 | Report the monthly completeness score and its trend | sales-operations | A0 | SAL-KPI-012 |
| 8 | Quarterly: review fields and picklists, retire unused ones, update this SOP | sales-operations | A0 | Field review |

## Decision rules
- **Placeholder values fail the audit:** "TBD", "n/a" in required fields, round-number amounts that never change, close dates on the last day of the year by default.
- A close date that has passed must be moved with a reason, or the deal closed.
- Agents may fill fields only from a source they can cite (a call summary, an email). They may not infer amounts or close dates.
- Merging records and deleting anything are human decisions (deletion is a [hard stop](https://github.com/et7admin/ai-agents-for-business/blob/main/manual/00-company-foundations/governance-and-autonomy.md#hard-stops) when it can't be undone).

## Quality checks
- [ ] The audit ran before every pipeline review.
- [ ] Each fix list went to the right owner.
- [ ] The completeness score is trending toward the target.
- [ ] No record was deleted without a human decision.

## Exceptions & escalation
| Situation | What to do | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|
| A seller's records are repeatedly incomplete | Raise it in their one-to-one | sales-manager |
| The CRM can't enforce a required field | Request a configuration change | head-of-it |
| A data-protection request arrives (access, deletion) | Don't handle it in sales; route it | legal-counsel |

## Records
Weekly audit reports and monthly scores, kept for at least a year to show the trend.
