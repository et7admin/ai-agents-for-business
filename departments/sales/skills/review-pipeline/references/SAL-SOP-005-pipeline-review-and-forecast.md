# SAL-SOP-005 Weekly pipeline review and forecast

## Purpose
Know every week whether the business will hit its sales target, which deals need help, and what to do about it. The meeting is for decisions on exceptions, not for reading the CRM aloud.

## Scope
**In:** all open opportunities at Stage 1 or later, the forecast for the current and next period, and coverage of future periods.
**Out:** individual coaching (one-to-ones), compensation, territory planning.

## Trigger
The weekly pipeline meeting. Inputs are prepared the working day before.

## Procedure

**Prepare (the day before)**

| # | Step | Role | Autonomy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Run the CRM hygiene audit and make sure owners have fixed what they can ([SAL-SOP-007](https://github.com/et7admin/ai-agents-for-business/blob/main/manual/sales/sops/SAL-SOP-007-crm-data-hygiene.md)) | sales-operations | A0 | Audit result |
| 2 | Build the snapshot: pipeline by stage and close period, coverage against remaining target, changes since last week (new, advanced, slipped, lost, amount changes) | sales-manager | A0 | Pipeline snapshot |
| 3 | Flag at-risk deals using the risk rules below | sales-manager | A0 | At-risk list |
| 4 | Each account-executive sets a forecast category for each of their deals closing this period | account-executive | A0 | Categories set |

**Meeting (30–60 minutes)**

| # | Step | Role | Autonomy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Review the numbers: coverage, commit versus target, change since last week (5 minutes) | sales-manager | A0 | Shared picture |
| 6 | Go through the at-risk deals and the largest commit deals. For each: what's true, what's missing, what we'll do. | sales-manager | A0 | Deal decisions |
| 7 | Record every action with an owner and a date | sales-manager | A1 | Action list |
| 8 | Call the forecast for the period: commit and best case | head-of-sales | A0 | Forecast call |

**After**

| # | Step | Role | Autonomy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Save a date-stamped forecast snapshot | sales-operations | A3 | Snapshot for SAL-KPI-008 |
| 10 | Send a short summary to leadership and finance: forecast, change, top risks, asks | sales-manager | A1 | Summary sent |

## Forecast categories
| Category | Meaning | Counts toward |
|---|---|---|
| **Commit** | The seller would bet on it closing this period: economic buyer agreed, paperwork path known, no open blockers (usually Stage 5) | Commit and best case |
| **Best case** | Could close this period if specific named things happen | Best case only |
| **Pipeline** | Real opportunity, but not this period or too early to say | Neither |
| **Omitted** | Shouldn't be in the forecast (stalled, unqualified) | Neither; fix or close it |

The **commit** number is the sum of commit deals, adjusted by the head-of-sales's judgment. Cross-check it against the **weighted pipeline** (amount × stage probability). A big gap between the two is itself a discussion item.

## Risk rules
Flag a deal when any of these are true:
- **Stalled:** no stage change for more than twice the median time deals spend in that stage (use 30 days until you have history).
- **Past due:** close date is in the past.
- **No next step,** or the next step date has passed.
- **Single-threaded:** only one contact engaged at Stage 3 or later.
- **Criteria gap:** the deal's stage exit criteria aren't actually met.
- **Pushed:** close date moved out two or more times.
- **Big change:** amount changed by more than 25% since last week.
- **Commit without evidence:** in commit but the economic buyer hasn't agreed.

## Decision rules
- A deal flagged three weeks running is either rescued with a specific plan this week or closed lost.
- Coverage below target (SAL-KPI-007) for the next period triggers a pipeline-generation plan agreed with marketing.
- The forecast changes only with a reason recorded ("deal X moved to best case because the economic buyer is on leave").

## Quality checks
- [ ] Hygiene audit ran before the meeting.
- [ ] Every at-risk deal was discussed or explicitly deferred.
- [ ] Every action has an owner and a date.
- [ ] The forecast snapshot was saved with a date.
- [ ] Leadership got the summary the same day.

## Exceptions & escalation
| Situation | What to do | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|
| Commit falls more than 10% below target | Present a gap-closing plan in the summary | head-of-sales → owner-ceo |
| A large deal is at risk | Plan an executive-sponsor call | head-of-sales |
| CRM data is too poor to forecast | Forecast from seller calls and record the data issue | sales-operations |

## Records
Snapshot, at-risk list, actions, forecast call and summary, kept in a dated log so forecast accuracy can be measured.
